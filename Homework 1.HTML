Albert Enstein:

Early Life

Albert Einstein was born in Germany in 1879. He grew up in Munich, loved mathematics, hated rigid schooling, and moved to Switzerland as a teenager.

Important Acheivements

In 1905, Einstein explained the photoelectric effect and E=mc². He later revolutionized physics by proving relativity, showing that gravity curves space and time.
